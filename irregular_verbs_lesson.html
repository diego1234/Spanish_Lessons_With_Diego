📖 Grammar: Present Tense Irregulars

Irregular verbs don't follow the regular -AR/-ER/-IR patterns. Here are the most important ones, grouped by ending, with present tense conjugations.

💡 Quick Tips / Consejos rápidos

  • SER vs ESTAR: SER = permanent traits ("Soy alto" - I am tall); ESTAR = temporary states/location ("Estoy cansado" - I am tired).
  • Many -ER/-IR irregulars only change in the "yo" form: hago, tengo, digo, vengo, pongo, salgo.
  • Stem-changing verbs like QUERER (e→ie) and PODER (o→ue) change in all forms except nosotros/vosotros.
  • SER (to be) is one of the most irregular verbs: soy, eres, es, somos, sois, son.
