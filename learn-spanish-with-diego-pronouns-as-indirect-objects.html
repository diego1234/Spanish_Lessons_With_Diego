Section 2

Grammar Discovery

Spanish has five indirect object pronouns. They answer: To whom? / For whom?

PronounEnglishExample
meto me / for meMamá me compra ropa.
teto you / for youYo te doy dinero.
leto him/herElla le explica la lección.
nosto us / for usNos cuentan una historia.
lesto them / for themMi padre les compra comida.

Why before the verb?

In Spanish, indirect object pronouns go before the conjugated verb:

Ella me da un libro. ← pronoun before verb
Ella da me un libro. ← wrong order!

Section 11

Common Mistakes

English speakers often make these errors. Avoid them!

Mistake 1: Wrong word order

Ella da me un libro.

Ella me da un libro.

The pronoun goes before the verb.

Mistake 2: Using \"le\" for everything

Le amo mucho. (meaning \"I love you\")

Te amo mucho.

Use te for \"you\" (informal), le for \"him/her.\"

Mistake 3: Forgetting the pronoun with \"gustar\"

Gusta el café. (meaning \"I like coffee\")

Me gusta el café.

\"Gustar\" needs a pronoun to say who likes it.

Mistake 4: Mixing up \"nos\" and \"les\"

Les damos las gracias. (meaning \"We thank you\")

Te damos las gracias.

Nos = us, les = them. Don't mix them!

Mistake 5: Repeating the pronoun

A mí me me gusta el chocolate.

Me gusta el chocolate.

Me already means \"to me.\" Don't double up.

Section 13

Review

Let's review everything we covered today.

The Five Pronouns

PronounMeaning
meto/for me
teto/for you
leto/for him/her
nosto/for us
lesto/for them

The Sentence Pattern

Subject→Pronoun→Verb→Thing

The pronoun always goes before the conjugated verb.

Five Key Rules

  1. Pronouns go before the conjugated verb: Ella me da un libro.
  2. Use me, te, le, nos, les — they don't change for gender.
  3. Le = him/her/you(formal). Use te for informal \"you.\"
  4. Pronouns are mandatory with verbs like gustar: Me gusta el café.
  5. For emphasis, add \"a + noun/pronoun\" after the verb: El libro es a mí.

Five Real-Life Examples

Me envían un paquete. — They send me a package.
Te recomiendo esta película. — I recommend this movie to you.
Le compré flores a mi mamá. — I bought flowers for my mom.
Nos dieron buenas noticias. — They gave us good news.
Les escribí una carta. — I wrote them a letter.
