Main Verb Grammar

Important verbs from the video — learn when and how to use each tense

Present Simple

Present Simple

Use it for habits, routines, and things that are always true. Something you do regularly.

I + base verb
I pray every morning.
She promises to help.
He makes good decisions.
EnglishI ___
prayI pray
promiseI promise
removeI remove
changeI change
respondI respond
chaseI chase
makeI make
Past Simple

Past Simple

Use it for finished actions in the past. Something that happened and is now over.

Subject + past verb
I prayed yesterday.
She changed her mind.
He chased the dog.

Present Perfect

Present Perfect

Use it for experiences that connect to the present. Something happened but the result matters now.

Have + past participle
I have prayed many times.
She has changed a lot.
He has made progress.
EnglishSpanish
I have prayedHe orado
I have changedHe cambiado
I have respondedHe respondido
I have made progressHe progresado
Future with WILL

Future with WILL

Use it for predictions, promises, or decisions made at the moment of speaking. Plans that feel certain.

Will + base verb
I will change tomorrow.
I will pray for you.
I will make better decisions.
