Why are irregular verbs important?
What makes a verb irregular?
Irregular verbs don't follow the standard -AR/-ER/-IR ending patterns. Their stems change in certain forms — making them essential to master for real conversation.

Los verbos irregulares no siguen los patrones estándar. Sus raíces cambian en ciertas formas.
