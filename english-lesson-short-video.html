4 · Grammar

Present → Past → Past participle

One verb, three moments: I do it, I did it, I have done it.

Present

I work every day.

Trabajo todos los días.

I wake up at 6 a.m.

Me despierto a las 6 a. m.

Past

I worked yesterday.

Ayer trabajé.

I woke up at 6 a.m.

Me desperté a las 6 a. m.

Past participle

I have worked here for two years.

He trabajado aquí durante dos años.

I have woken up early all week.

Me he despertado temprano toda la semana.

A simple rule: regular verbs add -ed (work → worked → worked). Irregular verbs change in other ways — like wake up → woke up → woken up — so you just need to learn them. Los verbos irregulares cambian su forma; hay que memorizarlos.
