💡 Grammar Tip

Indirect Object Pronouns — Before the Verb!

In Spanish, indirect object pronouns tell us WHO receives the action. They usually go directly before the conjugated verb. Think of them as answering "to whom?" or "for whom?"

👤 me — to/for me  |  te — to/for you  |  le — to/for him/her/you(formal)

👥 nos — to/for us  |  les — to/for them/you all

✅ "Te doy un regalo." → I give you a gift.

✅ "Nos compra pizza." → He buys us pizza.

💡 A2 Tip: Don't confuse with direct object pronouns (lo/la/los/las) — IOPs answer "to whom," not "what."
