Presente Simple

Describe hábitos, rutinas y hechos generales

¿Cuándo se usa?

  • Hábitos y rutinas diarias (I wake up at 7 every day)
  • Hechos generales y verdades (The sun rises in the east)
  • Horarios y planes fijos (The bus leaves at 8:30)
  • Situaciones permanentes (She lives in Madrid)

Estructura

Sujeto + Verbo base (+ s/es para he/she/it)
I wake up at 7 every day.
She brushes her teeth twice a day.
We go to work by bus.
He doesn't eat meat.
Do you study English?

Formación

Positivo: I/You/We/They + verbo | He/She/It + verbos
Negativo: I/You/We/They + don't + verbo | He/She/It + doesn't + verbo
Preguntas: Do + I/you/we/they + verbo? | Does + he/she/it + verbo?
🔄

Presente Continuo

Describe acciones que están ocurriendo ahora mismo

¿Cuándo se usa?

  • Acciones en este momento (I am eating breakfast right now)
  • Situaciones temporales (She is living in London this year)
  • Planes futuros confirmados (We are meeting them tomorrow)
  • Cambios en curso (The world is getting warmer)

Estructura

Sujeto + am/is/are + Verbo-ing
I am eating breakfast.
She is driving to work.
We are studying English right now.
They aren't watching TV.
Are you listening to me?

Formación

Positivo: I + am + V-ing | He/She/It + is + V-ing | We/You/They + are + V-ing
Negativo: am/is/are + not + V-ing
Preguntas: Am/Is/Are + sujeto + V-ing?
⏪

Pasado Simple

Describe acciones completadas en el pasado

¿Cuándo se usa?

  • Acciones completadas en un momento específico (I woke up at 7 yesterday)
  • Series de acciones en el pasado (She woke up, showered, and left)
  • Experiencias pasadas (We visited Paris last summer)
  • Conectores: yesterday, last week, ago, in 2020

Estructura

Sujeto + Verbo en pasado (V2)
I woke up at 7 yesterday.
She cooked dinner last night.
We went to the cinema last weekend.
He didn't call me.
Did you sleep well?

Formación

Positivo: Sujeto + V2 (pasado)
Negativo: Sujeto + didn't + verbo base
Preguntas: Did + sujeto + verbo base?
⏳

Pasado Continuo

Describe acciones en progreso en un momento del pasado

¿Cuándo se usa?

  • Acción en progreso interrumpida (I was sleeping when the phone rang)
  • Dos acciones simultáneas (She was cooking while I was cleaning)
  • Contexto/ambientación (The sun was shining and birds were singing)
  • Con when: interrupción | Con while: simultaneidad

Estructura

Sujeto + was/were + Verbo-ing
I was sleeping at 10 PM.
She was cooking dinner when I arrived.
They were watching TV while we were studying.
He wasn't paying attention.
Were you working late?

Uso de When y While

When + Pasado Simple: When I arrived, she was cooking.
While + Pasado Continuo: While I was reading, he was sleeping.
⏩

Futuro Simple

Describe predicciones, decisiones espontáneas y promesas

¿Cuándo se usa?

  • Predicciones (It will rain tomorrow)
  • Decisiones espontáneas (I'll have the chicken)
  • Promesas y ofertas (I will help you)
  • Planes con going to: (I'm going to travel next year)

Estructura

Sujeto + will + Verbo base
I will study tomorrow.
They will travel to Japan next year.
She will start her new job on Monday.
He won't forget your birthday.
Will you come to the party?
🔮

Futuro Continuo

Describe acciones que estarán en progreso en un momento del futuro

¿Cuándo se usa?

  • Acción en progreso en un momento futuro (I will be working at 3 PM tomorrow)
  • Acciones paralelas futuras (They will be sleeping while we travel)
  • Preguntas corteses (Will you be using the car tonight?)
  • Planes ya establecidos (I will be meeting the director at noon)

Estructura

Sujeto + will be + Verbo-ing
I will be working tomorrow at 3 PM.
They will be sleeping when we arrive.
She will be studying all night.
He won't be attending the meeting.
Will you be waiting for me?
